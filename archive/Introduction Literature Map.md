## 一、问题提出：漂绿为何重要

这一层用于说明：漂绿不是简单的“绿色宣传”，而是文本披露与实际绩效脱钩的治理问题，会影响信息环境、资本配置与企业价值。

### 核心文献

- Delmas, M. A., & Burbano, V. C. (2011). The drivers of greenwashing. *California Management Review, 54*(1), 64–87. https://doi.org/10.1525/cmr.2011.54.1.64
  - 用途：定义漂绿、解释合法性压力与组织动机。

- Lyon, T. P., & Maxwell, J. W. (2011). Greenwash: Corporate environmental disclosure under threat of audit. *Journal of Economics & Management Strategy, 20*(1), 3–41. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1530-9134.2010.00282.x
  - 用途：说明审计威胁会改变企业环境披露策略。

- Zhou, Y., Chen, L., Zhang, Y., & Li, W. (2024). “Environmental disclosure greenwashing” and corporate value: The premium effect and premium devalue of environmental information. *Corporate Social Responsibility and Environmental Management, 31*(3), 2424–2438. https://doi.org/10.1002/csr.2698
  - 用途：说明披露型漂绿具有资本市场后果。

- Baier, P., Berninger, M., & Kiesel, F. (2020). Environmental, social and governance reporting in annual reports: A textual analysis. *Financial Markets, Institutions & Instruments, 29*(3), 93–118. https://doi.org/10.1111/fmii.12132
  - 用途：说明年报 ESG 文本可被系统分析。

### 可补充文献

- Xu, C., Liu, J., Li, Z., & Lin, C. (2026). DeepGreen: Effective LLM-driven greenwashing monitoring system designed for empirical testing—Evidence from China. *Computational Economics*. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10614-026-11328-5
  - 用途：说明 LLM 漂绿识别正在提升测度精度。

- Pinarci, S. (2026). Disentangling greenwashing from authentic environmental performance: A double machine learning approach to corporate sustainability disclosure. *SSRN Electronic Journal*. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6710601
  - 用途：作为更先进测度方法的补充性过渡文献（若正式版接受工作论文）。

- Fang, X., Wang, Y., & Zhao, Z. (2025). Environmental regulations and the greenwashing of corporate ESG reports. *Economic Analysis and Policy*. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eap.2025.07.030
  - 用途：支持 ESG 报告层面的漂绿讨论。

## 二、方法动机：为什么 LLM 有必要进入漂绿测度

这一层用于说明：传统文本分析对语义理解有限，LLM 可以提升象征性披露与实质性披露的区分能力，从而让漂绿测度更可靠。

### 核心文献

- Baier, P., Berninger, M., & Kiesel, F. (2020). *Financial Markets, Institutions & Instruments*. https://doi.org/10.1111/fmii.12132
  - 用途：经典文本分析基线。

- Xu, C., Liu, J., Li, Z., & Lin, C. (2026). *Computational Economics*. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10614-026-11328-5
  - 用途：展示 LLM 识别绿色叙事和环境处罚的可能性。

- Bilinski, P. (2024). The usefulness of ChatGPT for textual analysis of annual reports. *SSRN Electronic Journal*. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4723503
  - 用途：说明 ChatGPT/LLM 已可用于年报文本分析。

- Zeng, Y. (2023). Textual analysis of annual reports by hedge funds. *SSRN Electronic Journal*. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4494834
  - 用途：提供年报文本分析的技术背景。

- Lilo, A., Paemurru, K., & Pärl, Ü. (2025). The textual representation of double materiality in ESG reports: Developing a content analysis codebook within a business model perspective. *Journal of Business Models, 13*(2), 55–74. https://doi.org/10.54337/jobm.v13i2.10050
  - 用途：说明 ESG 报告文本识别已向更复杂语义任务演进。

- Zhu, Y., Jin, X., & Sun, Y. (2025). Uncovering financial distress with textual risk disclosures in annual reports: Insights from large language models. *Information Systems Frontiers*. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10796-025-10637-8
  - 用途：说明 LLM 已能用于年报风险披露识别。

### 可补充文献

- Robb, A., & (search result suggests) additional annual-report textual analysis papers in 2025–2026 can be used only after DOI verification.

## 三、外部审计/鉴证为何可能影响非财务披露

这一层用于说明：审计并不只约束财务报表，也可能通过“其他信息”与鉴证机制影响 ESG/CSR 披露的可信度。

### 核心文献

- Cohen, J. R., & Simnett, R. (2015). CSR and assurance services: A research agenda. *Auditing: A Journal of Practice & Theory, 34*(1), 59–74. https://doi.org/10.2308/ajpt-50876
  - 用途：CSR 鉴证与审计研究的桥梁文献。

- Simnett, R., & Huggins, A. L. (2015). Integrated reporting and assurance: Where can research add value? *Sustainability Accounting, Management and Policy Journal, 6*(1), 29–53. https://doi.org/10.1108/SAMPJ-09-2014-0053
  - 用途：说明 assurance 可延伸到整合报告与非财务披露。

- Martínez-Ferrero, J., & García-Sánchez, I.-M. (2017). Sustainability assurance and cost of capital: Does assurance impact on credibility of corporate social responsibility information? *Business Ethics: A European Review, 26*(3), 223–239. https://doi.org/10.1111/beer.12152
  - 用途：证明可持续鉴证会影响 CSR 信息可信度。

- Martínez-Ferrero, J., Ruiz-Barbadillo, E., & Guidi, M. (2021). How capital markets assess the credibility and accuracy of CSR reporting: Exploring the effects of assurance quality and CSR restatement issuance. *Business Ethics, the Environment & Responsibility, 30*(4), 551–569. https://doi.org/10.1111/beer.12355
  - 用途：说明资本市场会区分 CSR 报告可信度与准确性。

- Bentley-Goode, K. A., Simnett, R., Thompson, A., & Trotman, A. J. (2025). Choice of assurance provider and impact on quality of sustainability reporting: Evidence from sustainability reporting restatements. *Accounting & Finance, 65*(2), 2135–2172. https://doi.org/10.1111/acfi.13367
  - 用途：说明鉴证提供者选择与可持续报告重述质量相关。

## 四、为什么“行业专长”是关键切入点

这一层用于说明：不是一般审计，而是行业专长更可能带来识别与约束效果。

### 核心文献

- Balsam, S., Krishnan, J., & Yang, J. S. (2003). Auditor industry specialization and earnings quality. *Auditing: A Journal of Practice & Theory, 22*(2), 71–97. https://doi.org/10.2308/aud.2003.22.2.71
  - 用途：行业专长提升审计质量的经典证据。

- Owhoso, D., Messier, W. F., & Lynch, J. G. (2002). Error detection by industry-specialized teams during sequential audit review. *Journal of Accounting Research, 40*(3), 883–900. https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-679X.00075
  - 用途：行业专门化团队错误识别更强。

- Reichelt, K. J., & Wang, D. (2010). National and city-level measures of auditor industry expertise and effects on audit quality. *Journal of Accounting Research, 48*(3), 647–686. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1475-679X.2009.00363.x
  - 用途：行业专长具有多层次结构。

- Rose-Green, E., Huang, H.-W., & Lee, C.-C. (2011). The association between auditor industry specialization and firms' disclosure of internal control weaknesses. *International Journal of Auditing, 15*(2), 204–216. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1099-1123.2011.00430.x
  - 用途：行业专长影响披露质量。

- Schmidt, J., & Wilkins, M. S. (2013). Bringing darkness to light: The influence of auditor quality and audit committee expertise on the timeliness of financial statement restatement disclosures. *Auditing: A Journal of Practice & Theory, 32*(1), 221–244. https://doi.org/10.2308/ajpt-50307
  - 用途：异常信息的识别与披露及时性。

- Francis, J. R. (2011). A framework for understanding and researching audit quality. *Auditing: A Journal of Practice & Theory, 30*(2), 125–152. https://doi.org/10.2308/ajpt-50006
  - 用途：搭建审计质量结构框架。

### 可补充文献

- Sun, J., & Liu, G. (2013). Auditor industry specialization, board governance, and earnings management. *Managerial Auditing Journal, 28*(1), 45–64. https://doi.org/10.1108/02686901311282498
  - 用途：行业专长与治理互动。

- Dunn, K. A., Mayhew, B. W., & Morsfield, S. G. (2000). Auditor industry specialization and client disclosure quality. *SSRN Electronic Journal*. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.213510
  - 用途：早期的披露质量证据，可作为补充。

- DeBoskey, D. G., & Jiang, W. (2012). The impact of auditor industry specialization on corporate transparency. *Research in Accounting Regulation, 24*(2), 108–116. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.racreg.2012.05.004
  - 用途：行业专长与透明度。

## 五、研究缺口：为什么还需要本文

这一层用于收束引言：已有研究分别解释了漂绿、审计专长、鉴证可信度，但尚未整合成“审计师行业专长是否抑制漂绿”的问题。

### 关键支撑文献

- Delmas & Burbano (2011)
- Balsam et al. (2003)
- Cohen & Simnett (2015)
- Martínez-Ferrero & García-Sánchez (2017)
- Xu et al. (2026)
- Baier et al. (2020)

### 缺口表述建议

- 漂绿研究主要集中于企业动机与披露偏离测度；
- 审计研究主要集中于财务质量与内控缺陷；
- ESG assurance 研究主要集中于鉴证可信度与资本市场反应；
- 但少有研究把“审计师行业专长—非财务披露约束—漂绿”连成闭环；
- 更少研究将这一闭环与 LLM 测度结合起来。

## 六、研究贡献：如果逻辑链成立，我们能学到什么

这一层用于引言末尾的贡献段。

### 可写成三点

1. **审计专业知识的外溢边界**
   - 行业专长不仅影响财务报表审计质量，也可能外溢到非财务信息治理。

2. **漂绿研究的治理视角**
   - 漂绿不只是企业内部动机问题，也受到外部监督与鉴证机制制约。

3. **方法与治理的结合**
   - LLM 改善了漂绿测度，而审计行业专长解释了漂绿为何会被约束。

### 可进一步强调

- 如果结果为负向关系，说明高质量外部审计对 ESG 文本治理具有真实约束作用；
- 如果结果不显著或方向不稳定，则说明行业专长在漂绿场景下存在边界，或背书效应与约束效应存在博弈。
